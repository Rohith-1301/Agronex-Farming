IMPORTANT:
These are separate, non-overlapping structured starter datasets (topic/field schemas), not a claim that every recommendation and FAQ answer has already been extracted from the TNAU webpages. The official pages could not be fetched in this file-generation session, so recommendation_or_answer and other fact fields are intentionally blank rather than filled with invented agronomic advice.

How to complete:
- Extract only information from the matching official source URL in each CSV.
- Keep each record in its matching domain. Do not copy tillage facts into nutrient management, etc.
- Populate source_page_or_section, source_publication_date, last_verified_date and reviewer.
- Record crop, district/agro-climatic zone, units, conditions and exact source citation for every recommendation.
- For pesticide/herbicide recommendations, verify current Indian registration and the product label before publishing.
- Have a qualified agronomist/TNAU/Department expert review high-impact recommendations.
- Publicly accessible webpages are not automatically licensed for unrestricted commercial reuse; check TNAU terms/copyright and obtain permission if needed.
